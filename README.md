\f0\fs24 \cf0 # AxioFlow Business \'97 Privacy Policy\
\
**Last Updated: August 26, 2026**\
\
AxioFlow Business ("App," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices regarding your data.\
\
---\
\
## 1. Information We Collect\
\
### Data You Create in the App\
\
- Business names, logos, and payment card images you add\
- Transactions (income, expenses, production, and custom types)\
- Categories, products and prices, inventory and stock locations\
- Customers, including names, phone numbers, addresses, and any map location you save\
- Orders, order items, delivery details, and payments\
- Scheduled transactions and app settings\
\
**This data is stored locally on your device.** We operate no servers, and we cannot see it. It is not transmitted to us, and it is not sold or shared, except through the specific services listed in Section 3.\
\
### Camera & Photo Library\
\
If you add a business logo or a payment card image, the App asks for access to your camera or photo library. Images you choose are stored with your business data on your device and are never uploaded to us. Payment card images may show bank or e-wallet account details, so treat them as you would any other financial document \'97 they are included in your backups and appear on receipts you share.\
\
### Location & Maps\
\
If you choose to save a location for a customer, the App asks for your device location and uses map features. This is optional, only happens when you tap to use it, and is not background tracking.\
\
To show the map and turn coordinates into a readable address, the App contacts third-party services and sends them the relevant coordinates or search text:\
\
- **OpenStreetMap Nominatim** \'97 address search and reverse geocoding\
- **OpenStreetMap tile servers** \'97 map imagery\
- **unpkg.com** \'97 the map library the App loads\
\
These requests carry the coordinates or the address text you are searching for, along with your IP address, as any web request does. They do not carry your business, transaction, or customer records. If you never save a customer location, the App does not contact them.\
\
### iCloud Sync (Pro Users Only)\
\
If you subscribe to AxioFlow Pro and enable iCloud Sync, your data is synced across your Apple devices using **Apple iCloud**, and backups are stored there. This data is governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). It goes to your own iCloud account. We do not have access to it.\
\
### Automatically Collected Information\
\
- **Advertising Data:** The free Starter plan displays ads via Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers, IP address, and usage data for personalized or contextual advertising. iOS asks your permission before tracking. See [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).\
- **Purchase Data:** Payment for AxioFlow Pro is processed entirely by Apple through In-App Purchase. We do not collect or store your payment information. See [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).\
- **Crash & Diagnostic Data:** We may use Apple's built-in crash reporting. No personally identifiable information is collected through this.\
\
## 2. How We Use Your Information\
\
- To provide and maintain the App's features\
- To process your subscription via Apple In-App Purchase\
- To display advertisements on the free Starter plan\
- To sync and back up your data via iCloud, if you enable it\
- To show maps and addresses, if you use customer locations\
- To send you local reminders and summaries, if you enable notifications\
\
Notifications are scheduled on your device. They are not sent from a server and their content does not leave your phone.\
\
## 3. Data Sharing\
\
We do **not** sell, trade, or share your personal data with third parties, except as described below:\
\
| Service | Purpose | Data Shared |\
|---------|---------|-------------|\
| Apple (In-App Purchase) | Subscription billing | Payment handled by Apple |\
| Apple (iCloud) | Sync and backup across your devices | Your app data, in your own iCloud account |\
| Google AdMob | Advertising (free plan) | Device identifiers, usage data |\
| OpenStreetMap (Nominatim, tiles) | Customer map and address lookup | Coordinates or search text, IP address |\
| unpkg.com | Loading the map library | IP address |\
\
Anything you share yourself \'97 a receipt image or an exported spreadsheet \'97 goes wherever you send it, and is no longer covered by this policy.\
\
## 4. Data Storage & Security\
\
- App data is stored **locally on your device** in an on-device database.\
- iCloud-synced data is encrypted and managed by Apple.\
- We do not operate any servers that store your data.\
- We do not have access to your financial, customer, or transaction data.\
\
## 5. Children's Privacy\
\
The App is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can delete it.\
\
## 6. Your Rights & Choices\
\
- **Delete your data:** You can delete any business, transaction, customer, or all app data from within the App at any time.\
- **Disable iCloud Sync:** You can turn off iCloud Sync in the App's settings.\
- **Skip location:** Saving a customer location is optional. You can decline the permission, type an address by hand, or remove a saved location at any time.\
- **Ad Tracking:** You can limit ad tracking in your device settings (Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking on iOS).\
- **Notifications:** You can turn reminders off in the App's settings or in iOS Settings.\
- **Uninstall:** Removing the App deletes all locally stored data. Data already synced to your iCloud account is removed through iCloud.\
\
## 7. Third-Party Links\
\
The App may contain links to external websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those third parties.\
\
## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy\
\
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by the "Last Updated" date at the top. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.\
